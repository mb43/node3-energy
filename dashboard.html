ℹ HOW THE NUMBERS WORK: GRID P&L = Real cash. Export income − charge cost. What Octopus pays Dovecote for grid exports minus what Octopus charges to charge the battery. This is the actual bank account movement — no modelling, no assumptions. The battery also covers the home's 12kWh/day consumption from its charge cycles, meaning the homeowner pays £0 for electricity up to that cap. The Grid P&L proves the system is commercially viable even after providing that free electricity. HOME ENERGY (indicative) = The 12kWh/day the battery provides to the home free of charge, valued at 25p/kWh SVT reference. Shown for context only — the real homeowner benefit is simply £0/day electricity cost up to the 12kWh fair-use cap, not a tariff comparison. NET VALUE = Grid P&L + Home Energy value. Combined annual benefit to the household. The chart above plots this.
