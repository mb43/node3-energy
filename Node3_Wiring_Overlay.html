⛔ CORRECTIONS APPLIED 15 Sep 2026 — READ BEFORE WIRING

Three errors found in original document and corrected:
1. CAN-A/B were swapped. LEAF battery → CAN-B. FoxESS → CAN-A. (Previously had LEAF on CAN-A, FoxESS on CAN-B via Waveshare HAT — both wrong.)
2. GPIO pins were wrong. Correct: GPIO 21 = precharge, GPIO 17 = negative, GPIO 48 = positive. (Previously showed GPIO 25/32/33 — those are T-CAN485/DevKit pins, not T-2CAN.)
3. Waveshare CAN HAT is NOT required. T-2CAN is galvanically isolated on both channels natively. FoxESS connects directly to T-2CAN CAN-A. Source: DALA wiki.
