⚠ HIGH VOLTAGE DC — 360–403V nominal. A shock at this voltage is lethal. Class 0 (1000V) gloves, insulated tools, one-handed working, buddy + E-stop plan before any pack is connected. Never work alone on energised DC.

Full HV + LV topology: TOMZN master/branch breakers, Service Disconnect Plugs, 3× Nissan e-NV200 packs in parallel, FoxESS KH10.5. For the LV-only control/comms wiring in isolation, use the tab above.

12V rail, E-stop, LilyGo T-2CAN, SSR-04, CAN-A/CAN-B and the Yazaki daisy chain only — HV busbar/breakers deliberately omitted here for clarity. See "Connection Tables" tab for the pin-by-pin wiring list.

The T-2CAN has two on-board CAN channels. The Waveshare isolated HAT sits between CAN-B and the FoxESS for galvanic isolation — the FoxESS HV2600 CAN can float at up to ±110V DC to PE and would destroy an unisolated transceiver (and could injure you). This isolator is mandatory, not optional.

Each SSR-04 OUT switches 12V from the same rail that feeds Pin 1. Sequence (driven by LilyGo firmware): precharge ON → wait → +cont ON → −cont ON → precharge OFF. Each pack's internal BMS sees its own coil signal arrive and operates its own internal contactor accordingly.

Safety chain — what opens the circuit

FaultWhat happens
Single cell overvoltage in any packThat pack's own BMS opens its internal contactors. Other 2 packs stay live. In single-BMS-read mode, LilyGo won't directly see a Pack 2/3 fault — relies on that pack's own protection.
LilyGo crash / firmware faultSSR drive signals go LOW → coil voltage drops → all contactors open. Failsafe by design.
12V rail lossPin 1 BAT+IGN drops → BMSes go to sleep → contactors open.
Mains AC lossDR-60-12 has ~50ms hold-up, then 12V drops, then contactors open. Whole bus dies safely.
Person needs to disconnect NOWE-stop — drops Pin 1 across all 3 packs simultaneously, ~100ms.
Service workPull Service Disconnect Plugs (with HV gloves). Each SDP physically breaks the cell string.
Branch fault on one packThat pack's TOMZN branch breaker trips. Other 2 carry on.
Inverter faultMaster TOMZN trips manually, or FoxESS opens its internal DC contactor.
